
## 3. Resources

Resource gathering is a major part of gameplay, especially early on. A few clear rules:

- Mining ores or gathering materials is discouraged in community areas (spawn chunks, shopping district, etc.).
- Light up caves and dark underground areas to minimize unwanted mob spawns.
- Players may not monopolize a unique resource, especially rarer ones like elytra. If you mass-collect something, consider opening a shop for it to help other players.
- Some unique areas are planned to regenerate periodically for easier resource gathering — for example, a server mining desert.

## 4. Farms & Automation

To keep the server lag-friendly and the economy balanced, here are the rules on farms:

- You can build any farm you like — but if you don't need a material in bulk, or someone else already stocks it, consider not building a duplicate farm for it.
- Farms should be built where they won't interfere with other players' areas. If your farm will be close to another player's base, ask for their consent first (smaller-scale farms are still allowed regardless — check with an admin if you're unsure).
- AFK farms, mob farms, and redstone contraptions are all allowed, as long as they don't cause server-side or client-side lag outside their own area.
- Chunk loaders of any kind are currently prohibited unless coordinated with the admins.
- TNT-based machines, item duplicators, and other exploit-based farms are allowed but strongly discouraged.
- Using someone else's farm without consent is prohibited, even a simple AFK farm.

## 5. Villagers!

Villagers are essential to many farms, but large numbers of them cause lag. A few rules to help:

- You can technically build a villager breeder anywhere, but in a community space it should be temporary and removed afterward.
- If your villagers don't need to move, use the provided tools (plugins/datapacks — see the Tools section) to disable their AI and reduce lag.
- Avoid hoarding villagers for trades you rarely use — use the server trading hall for those, and build your own hall around the trades you actually need often.
- If you start a community project involving villagers (especially trading halls), make sure it's usable by other players — easy access for zombification/curing, etc.

## 6. Economy

Every good SMP needs an economy. We keep the rules loose, but with a few constraints:

- This server runs on a diamond-based economy. By default, shops are expected to price in diamonds unless it's a special case.
- All player shops should be located in the designated shopping district.
- Set up a dedicated shop building/structure for each item (or category of items) you sell.
- It's suggested you make your shop thematic to what it sells.
- You're welcome to team up with other players to build a shop for multiple items.
- Multiple shops for the same item are technically allowed but discouraged if one already exists.
- Respect the shopping district and other players' work — no stealing or taking items from shops without paying (pranks are still fine).
- Contribute to the district's visual appeal — a shop alone isn't 'done.' Add surroundings and infrastructure around it.
- If you're unsure about anything regarding your shop, check with an admin before building.
- Selling off your excess items is strongly encouraged — it helps other players and keeps the economy moving.
- As noted in the Player ↔ Player section, monopolizing a single item is prohibited.

## 7. PvP and Combat

- Competitive or harsh PvP (traps, TNT, etc.) is prohibited unless both sides consent.
- If a prank ends up killing someone, make sure to save their items for them.

## 8. The Nether

- A nether hub is planned for the future. For now, please keep the nether clean and avoid random block vomit.

## 10. Technical Minecraft

- Large amounts of redstone components in community areas are prohibited unless cleared with the admins.
- Chunk loaders of any kind are prohibited unless cleared with the admins.
- Build redstone contraptions to be lag-friendly — avoid always-on clocks and anything else that hurts server performance. Violations will be addressed.

## 11. Mods and Resource Packs

- We won't force you to avoid mods or resource packs that give an unfair advantage, but we strongly encourage you not to use them at others' expense.
- Visual mods and resource packs are completely fine to use.
- Auto-clickers and other input modifiers are allowed for AFK farms and similar setups; other uses are discouraged.
- Any type of hacked client or cheat is prohibited.

## 12. Chat

- Keep chat respectful — harassment of any kind is not allowed.
- Avoid spamming, advertising, or drama as much as possible.
- Don't disturb players who don't want to be disturbed, especially via the voice chat mod.

## 13. Multiplayer Co-op

- Teams are a thing! Cooperate with others, build something useful, and grow the world together.
- If you're building something with another player, agree on ownership of the project beforehand.
- If you share a project with someone, both of you may use it according to your agreement.
- If you share a shop with someone, profits must be split between you.
- Project shares can be bought and sold.
